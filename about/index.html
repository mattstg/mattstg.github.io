About me

I'm Matt, a game developer in Montreal. At heart I'm an engine and tools developer: I like building the systems that make everyone else on a team faster.

I picked up Unity early, years before schools taught it, which is how I ended up teaching it and then running a game development program at a private college. I loved teaching, and it made me good at explaining systems and bringing new people up to speed.

For the last two years I've been building tools for a new kind of teammate: AI agents. I like that work for the same reason I like tools: it takes the tedious parts off developers' hands so they can spend their time on the game.

What I'm looking for

A senior Unity, game tools or AI tooling role on a game team. I'm in Montreal and open to remote roles with US teams.
