Work/Legends of Learning

Legends of Learning

Five educational games I built under contract and architected the core systems of, four of which I also designed. Teachers assign them in classrooms on Chromebooks, the web and iPads, and both teachers and students rate them.

Legends of Learning gave us only the learning goal, for example "teach animal reproductive strategies and the difference between R and K strategies". The game itself, its concept, gameplay and how it teaches, was ours to design. I designed Malfunction, Educagent, Bid for Life and Dance of Atoms, and playtested every game by hand throughout development.
