ISI (Institut supérieur d'informatique), 2018 – 2023

Collège Universel bought ISI in December 2022; I kept running the program there through 2023.

  • Wrote the curriculum for the program's 7 game courses.
  • Taught about 12 different courses: Unity, Unreal, C#, C++, VR, online multiplayer (RPC, Photon) and engine tools, including a lesson on Unity visual scripting.
  • Managed 4–5 teachers, and onboarded new teachers across the school as it grew fivefold, from about 300 to 1,500 students.
  • Supervised 30+ multi-month student game projects run with Agile Scrum.
  • Helped organize a game jam at the school.
  • Taught in both English and French, and coordinated with the administration.
  • While the school expanded, also acted as temporary head of the English general programming program until a head was hired: updated and translated its material, helped train new teachers, and taught its first six courses on tight deadlines.

Hundreds of students in project-based courses taught me to onboard and manage juniors, write thorough documentation, and debug the problems only juniors manage to create.
